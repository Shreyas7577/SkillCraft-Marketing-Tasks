## 3. Deep Dive Rationale

### **A. Primary Choice: Alexandra Saint Mieux**
* **Follower Demographics:** 85% Female, Ages 18–30. Locations: USA, UK, and France.
* **Why it works:** She recently went viral for Rhode’s "Lip Case" campaign. Her content isn't just a "review"—it's an aesthetic lifestyle.
* **Engagement Math:** With an 8.9% engagement rate on TikTok, she generates nearly $3 \times$ the platform average, ensuring the brand stays "trending."

### **B. Secondary Choice: Monet McMichael**
* **Follower Demographics:** High concentration of Gen Z (Ages 16–24), Diverse/Multicultural audience.
* **Why it works:** Monet is the queen of "Community Influence." When she uses a product in a vlog, it often sells out within 24 hours due to her high authenticity score.
